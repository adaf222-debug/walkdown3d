Walkdown 3D

Privacy policy

Effective 12 November 2026

Walkdown 3D is an Android app for viewing 3D models offline. It is made by Paseko Dev., an individual developer ("we"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

In short: the app collects nothing and sends nothing. Everything you do in it stays on your device.

What the app collects

Nothing. The app has no account or sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no crash reporting, and there is no server it talks to. It does not ask Android for internet access, so it cannot send anything over the network itself. We never receive your models, notes, photos, measurements or location.

Separately from the app, Android and Google Play may collect crash and performance reports about the apps on your device, depending on your device's settings, and Google shows developers summaries of them in Google Play Console (Android vitals). This is done by Android and Google under Google's privacy policy, not by the app, and these reports do not tell us who you are.

What stays on your device

  • Models you import, and the packages the app makes from them.
  • Notes, saved views, markups and photos you add to a model.
  • Settings, and which version of these terms you accepted and when.
  • When you first opened the app, for the free trial, and Google Play's signed record of your Full version purchase, if you buy it.

These are kept in the app's own storage on your device. Uninstalling the app deletes them, so export your notes first if you want to keep them. Snapshots you save go to your device's Pictures folder, where your gallery app shows them and any photo backup you have turned on may copy them. They stay until you delete them.

Things you choose to share

When you use Export or Share, the app hands the file to Android, and you choose where it goes, such as a folder, email or messaging app. What happens next is up to you and that app. Nothing is shared without you doing it.

Send feedback opens your email app with a message to us that already holds the app's name, version and build, the device's maker and model, the Android version and the amount of memory, to help us understand problems. You can change or delete any of it, and nothing is sent unless you press send. We use your email only to reply to you and to fix the app.

Emails you send us

You do not have to write to us. No law requires it, and the app works the same if you never do. If you email us, through Send feedback or otherwise, we receive your email address, your message and anything in it, and we are responsible for that information (its controller). We use it only to answer you, to fix the app and, if you write about a purchase, to deal with it, because we have a legitimate interest in answering you and improving the app. Emails are kept in our Gmail account, which Google provides, and are deleted 2 years after the last message in the conversation, unless the law requires us to keep them longer. Google may store them on servers outside your country, including outside Israel and the European Economic Area, under the safeguards in Google's own terms. We do not use them for marketing and do not share them with anyone else.

You can ask us for a copy of what we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it, or to stop using it, by writing to the contact address below. If you are in Israel, this includes your rights to see and correct information about you under the Protection of Privacy Law, 5741-1981, and you can complain to the Privacy Protection Authority. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland and are not happy with our answer, you can complain to the data protection authority in the country where you live or work.

Permissions

  • Notifications, to show the progress of a long model conversion. You can decline it and the conversion still runs.
  • Running in the foreground and keeping the device awake, so a conversion is not stopped halfway.
  • Google Play billing, so Full version can be bought through the Google Play app on your device.
  • Seeing whether the device has a network connection, which Google's billing software asks for. It does not give the app internet access.

Photos are taken through your device's own camera app and chosen through Android's own picker, so the app does not ask for access to your camera or your gallery. Files are opened through Android's own file picker, which gives the app only the file you choose.

Purchases

Full version is bought through Google Play. Google handles the payment and your payment details under Google's privacy policy, and we do not see your card or bank details. The app only asks the Google Play app on your device whether Full version has been bought, and keeps Google's signed answer on your device so that it keeps working with no signal. Google may share order details with us through Google Play Console, as it does for every app sold on Google Play, and we use them only for our records, tax and refunds.

Children

The app is meant for adults working with 3D models and is not aimed at children. Since it collects no information, it collects none from children either.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever starts handling information differently, this page will be updated before that version is released, with a new effective date.
