Walkdown 3D

Privacy policy

Effective 9 November 2026

Walkdown 3D is an Android app for viewing 3D models offline. It is made by Paseko Dev., an individual developer ("we"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

In short: the app collects nothing and sends nothing. Everything you do in it stays on your device.

What the app collects

Nothing. The app has no account or sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no crash reporting, and there is no server it talks to. It does not ask Android for internet access, so it cannot send anything over the network itself. We never receive your models, notes, photos, measurements or location.

What stays on your device

  • Models you import, and the packages the app makes from them.
  • Notes, saved views, markups and photos you add to a model.
  • Settings, and the date the app was first opened, which is used for the free trial.

These are kept in the app's own storage on your device. Uninstalling the app deletes them, so export your notes first if you want to keep them. Snapshots you save go to your device's Pictures folder, where they stay until you delete them.

Things you choose to share

When you use Export or Share, the app hands the file to Android, and you choose where it goes, such as a folder, email or messaging app. What happens next is up to you and that app. Nothing is shared without you doing it.

Permissions

  • Notifications, to show the progress of a long model conversion. You can decline it and the conversion still runs.
  • Running in the foreground and keeping the device awake, so a conversion is not stopped halfway.

Photos are taken through your device's own camera app and chosen through Android's own photo picker, so the app does not ask for access to your camera or your gallery. Files are opened through Android's own file picker, which gives the app only the file you choose.

Purchases

The app's one-time purchase, Full version, is made through Google Play. Google handles the payment and your payment details under Google's privacy policy. We do not see your card or bank details. The app only asks Google Play on your device whether the purchase has been made.

Children

The app is meant for adults working with 3D models and is not aimed at children. Since it collects no information, it collects none from children either.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever starts handling information differently, this page will be updated before that version is released, with a new effective date.
